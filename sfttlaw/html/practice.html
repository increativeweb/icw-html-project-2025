Practice

We are sensitive to our clients’ financial situations and we are able to provide flexible and innovative fee arrangements.

Strategic Business and Financial Advice

While our offices are located in the nation’s capital, Washington, D.C., our clients are located throughout the United States. We represent clients in a broad range of industries, from financial services, with assets ranging from $25 million to $50 billion, to investment banks, private equity firms, manufacturers, retailers, distributors, medical practices, technology firms and restaurant chains.

Our clients receive the direct, personal attention of the engagement partner who is responsible for making sure that the client’s requests are met on a timely basis at a fair and reasonable fee. We understand the business of each of our clients, permitting us to deliver valuable strategic business and financial advice in connection with our legal services.

Results Oriented

We are a results-oriented firm whose focus is to be part of the solution to achieve our client’s goals and encourage an open line of communication with our clients and client feedback on any aspect of our relationship. We work directly with Boards of Directors and/or senior management on matters of extreme importance to the corporate life of our clients. Our goal is to support and assist in-house counsel (if one exists) and to work closely with local counsel to achieve the clients’ desired results.

Our clients prize us for the depth of our knowledge, our personal attention to the details of their deals and our ability to work through seemingly endless and complicated issues to achieve the desired goals.

PRACTICES

Corporate and Securities

We counsel our clients on corporate laws and regulations and all aspects of corporate governance and board matters, including fiduciary responsibilities.

Mergers and Acquisitions

We represent our clients on the buy-side or on the sell-side of mergers and acquisitions and have represented a variety of companies on these transactions.

Financial Institutions

We are an established nationwide leader in handling significant and complex transactional and regulatory matters for commercial banks, savings institutions and financial institution holding companies.

Mutual To Stock Conversions/Mutual Holding Companies

We are one of the most experienced law firms in the country in mutual to stock conversions and mutual holding company transactions.

Employee Benefits and Compensation

We have substantial expertise in advising public and private businesses and individuals in all aspects of qualified and unqualified employee benefit plans.

Regulatory Matters

For over 40 years, we have represented companies before the Federal Reserve Board, the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, the FDIC, the SEC, and various state regulatory agencies.

Intellectual Property And Tax

We serve as outside counsel to both public and private companies, and we represent individuals, estates, trusts and tax-exempt organizations.

Start-ups: Private Finance/venture Capital

We advise privately held companies on business entity choice and on the structure, negotiation and preparation of capital formation transactions.

Corporate Defense

We assist management with corporate defense planning, tactics and deployment.

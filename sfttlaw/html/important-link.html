PRACTICES

Corporate and Securities

We counsel our clients on corporate laws and regulations and all aspects of corporate governance and board matters, including fiduciary responsibilities.

Mergers and Acquisitions

We represent our clients on the buy-side or on the sell-side of mergers and acquisitions and have represented a variety of companies on these transactions.

Financial Institutions

We are an established nationwide leader in handling significant and complex transactional and regulatory matters for commercial banks, savings institutions and financial institution holding companies.

Mutual To Stock Conversions/Mutual Holding Companies

We are one of the most experienced law firms in the country in mutual to stock conversions and mutual holding company transactions.

Employee Benefits and Compensation

We have substantial expertise in advising public and private businesses and individuals in all aspects of qualified and unqualified employee benefit plans.

Regulatory Matters

For over 40 years, we have represented companies before the Federal Reserve Board, the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, the FDIC, the SEC, and various state regulatory agencies.

Intellectual Property And Tax

We serve as outside counsel to both public and private companies, and we represent individuals, estates, trusts and tax-exempt organizations.

Start-ups: Private Finance/venture Capital

We advise privately held companies on business entity choice and on the structure, negotiation and preparation of capital formation transactions.

Corporate Defense

We assist management with corporate defense planning, tactics and deployment.

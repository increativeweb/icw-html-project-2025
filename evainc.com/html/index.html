Who We Are

EVA’s unmatched success in guiding clients to sound investment and operational decisions stems from the outstanding capabilities of our expert consultants and analysts, coupled with the unique hands-on approach of our firm. Because EVA is a smaller company than most energy consulting conglomerates, we provide a far more personalized, focused, interactive and responsive experience for our clients and customers.

About EVA

Energy Ventures Analysis (EVA) unmatched success in guiding clients to sound investment and operational decisions stems from the outstanding capabilities of our expert consultants and analysts, coupled with the unique hands-on approach of our firm. Because EVA is a smaller company than most energy consulting conglomerates, we provide a far more personalized, focused, interactive and responsive experience for our clients and customers.

Every project is different and has its own set of challenges. By working with clients across the entire energy sector, EVA is uniquely positioned to help its clients solve their most complex problems in a rapidly changing industry.

Today’s ever-changing global energy market requires a partner who will provide an uncensored and comprehensive view. Since 1981, EVA has offered industry-leading subscription services across the entire landscape of energy markets.

Learn more about EVA’s world renowned consultants, and explore the diverse array of market data products that EVA offers in the Electric Power, Natural Gas & Oil, Coal, Renewable Energy and Environmental sectors.

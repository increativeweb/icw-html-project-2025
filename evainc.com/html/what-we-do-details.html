Quarterly Power Report

EVA’s Quarterly Power Report delivers expert insights on U.S. power markets, trends, forecasts, and pricing dynamics.

Quarterly Power Repot

EVA presents the Quarterly Power Report, the premier resource for navigating the intricate dynamics of the U.S. power markets.

Our meticulously curated analysis offers a comprehensive review of recent and impending market shifts and policy evolutions at a power market level, augmented by a nuanced examination of capacity, generation, and commodity pricing trends. Each quarter, we deliver our expertly formulated base case view, leveraging sophisticated integrated modeling and price projections. Furthermore, our report monitors pivotal state and federal regulatory landscapes, alongside the trajectory of various decarbonization initiatives, ensuring stakeholders are equipped with the latest insights to navigate the evolving grid adeptly. Even for professionals operating in spheres of gas, coal, renewables or environmental advocacy, this power report stands as an indispensable tool for remaining abreast of the critical factors shaping market dynamics.

Each report contains historical data and a short-term outlook while showcasing:

  • Comprehensive review of recent and forthcoming market and policy developments in U.S. power markets.
  • Short-term outlook on electricity demand, capacity, generation, and commodity prices for all major power markets.
  • An expert view on capacity markets and prices.
  • Pivotal state and federal regulatory landscapes.
  • Tracking the trajectory of various decarbonization initiatives at the state and federal levels.

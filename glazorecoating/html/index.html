About Us

Enhance your Products with Glazore Coating

Excellence in surface coating solutions.

Glazore Coating Pvt. Ltd. has built a reputation as a prominent player in providing high end surface treatment solutions of aluminium profiles designed for industrial applications as well as contemporary infrastructure.

Drawing on years of industry expertise in aluminium extrusion, we are committed to promoting innovation and delivering excellent value added solutions within the aluminium sector, offering one stop high-quality solutions to clients around the globe.

Founded in 2026, our operations are based in Harsol, Sabarkantha, Gujarat over 65000 sq ft area. Our state-of-the-art facility features advance machinery and automated systems along with advanced quality control equipments allowing us to deliver precision surface treatment solutions to our customer.

Your Gift Supports TYO’s Emergency Response

Lifesaving Emergency Aid

Food, water, hygiene items, winter/survival supplies, transportation, urgent family assistance, and direct support for households facing immediate crisis.

Mental Health & Medical Care

Psychological first aid, counseling, case management, caregiver support, referrals, and support for children and families coping with trauma, grief, fear, and instability.

Safe Learning & Protection

Safe spaces, educational materials, structured learning, play, social-emotional support, and continued connection to trusted adults when schools are closed, damaged, or unsafe.

Why give through TYO?

  • 18 years rooted in Palestine
  • Emergency-ready local team
  • Holistic child and family support
